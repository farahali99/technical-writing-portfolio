# Slack FAQ
## What is Slack?
Slack is a business communication platform that helps teams collaborate through channels, direct messages, and integrations with other tools.
Slack helps teams work more efficiently.
## Is Slack free to use?
Yes, Slack offers a free plan with limited features in several categories, as follows:
- Working with external people (one-to-one).
- Use audio and video.
- Canvases (channels and DM only).
- Integrations with other apps.
- User management with SCIM provisioning.
< **Note:**
< Additional features are available with a paid plan, such as Pro, Business+, and Enterprise+.
  ## How do I create a workspace?
  To create a workspace, follow these steps:
- Go to the Slack website.
- Enter your email address.
- Click **continue**, or you can continue with Google or Apple.
- Check your email for a **confirmation code**.
- Enter your code, then click **create a workspace**.
- Follow the prompts.
  ## How do I send a direct message?
  To send a direct message in Slack, do the following:
- Log in to your Slack workspace.
- From the side par **click the + icon**.
- **Select the person** you want to message.
- Type your message, or add your attachments, emojis, and formatting.
- Click **Enter** to send the message.
> **Note:**
> You can click the arrow icon next to the paper plane to schedule your message for later.
## Can I share my files in Slack?
Yes, you can share your files on Slack by:
- Open the channel or direct message where you want to share the file.
- **Select the file** you want to share.
- Click the **Share icon**.
- Choose where to share the file.
- Click **Share**.
 ## How do I reset my password?
 To reset your password, do the following:
 - Open the Slack **sign-in page**.
 - Select **Forgot Password**.
 - Enter your **email address**.
 - Check your inbox for a *Slack confirmation code*.
 - **Enter the code**.
 - Follow the instructions sent to your email.
> **Note:**
> To reset your password, you must have access to the email address associated with your Slack account.
