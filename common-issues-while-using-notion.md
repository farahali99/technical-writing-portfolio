# Common issues when using Notion

This guide describes common issues in Notion and provides steps to resolve them.
## Cannot create a new page 

You may be unable to create a new page for several reasons.

### Why this issue may occur:
- Reaching the free plan’s block limit.
- You added extra members.
- You have permission to view only.
- Temporary software or loading issues.

### What you can try to fix this issue:
To fix this issue:
- Remove some members from your workspace.
- Change the workspace permission to “edit” or “full access."
- Log out and log back in to your account to fix loading issues.
- Click the “+ new page” button at the bottom of the sidebar.
- Type “page” within an existing page to create a new page.

## Cannot share a page
Notion pages are private by default.

### Why can't I share a page?
This may occur because of:
- The page is set to private.
- Reach the workspace's free guest limit.
- Permission is restricted by workspace admin, so you're not allowed to share pages or invite guests.
- The parent page above your subpage that you want to share might not be shared or accessible.
- Network or browser issues.

### What you can try to fix this issue:
To fix it:
- Click Share at the top right.
- Make sure “share to web” is active.
- Enter the email address correctly.
- Click "publish."
- Go to settings & members to see if you have reached your plan’s guest limit.
- Try another browser or an incognito tab.
- Click “...” in the sidebar to move the page from the private folder to the workspace.

## Cannot upload a photo or file
You can't add photos or files due to the 5MB file size limit on the free plan.

### Why can't I add a photo and files
 This may occur because of:
- The size of files and images exceeded the maximum limit in the free plan.
- Browsers prevent new uploads.
- You don't have permission to edit.
- The Notion app is outdated.
- Browser and network issues.

### What you can try to fix this issue:
To fix this error:
- Upload images and files to Google Drive, Dropbox, and OneDrive.
- Compress images and files to PDF.
- Use an incognito tab or clear your browser cache.
- Ask the workspace admin for editing permission.
- Update your Notion desktop app.
- Check your internet connection.
- Add a property named “files & media” in a database to upload images and files directly into table cells.
- Type “image” in the editor, and select upload to upload images directly from your computer.

## I can't create a list and a table

You must know the difference between tables and databases.
 Tables are a basic layout, like a spreadsheet in Word.
 Databases can filter, sort, and show data in different views, like board and calendar.

### Why this problem may occur
This error occurs due to the following: 
- The page is locked.
- Technical glitch.
- Network issues.

### What can you try to fix this problem 
To fix this issue:
- Click the three-dot menu “...” in the top right corner.
- Toggle “lock page."
- Type “-” or “*” to make a list.
- Type “Table” and select “table—inline" for a flexible table.
- Click the six-dot menu to turn a database into a simple table.
